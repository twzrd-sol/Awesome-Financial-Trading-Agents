# Awesome-Financial-Trading-Agents

## AI Financial Trading Agents Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on AI Agents for Financial Trading & Investment*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** building **AI financial trading agents** — autonomous or semi-autonomous systems that perform market research, sentiment analysis, technical/fundamental evaluation, risk management, portfolio optimization, and trade execution.

**Examples** include BlackRock Aladdin, TradingAgents (GitHub), Deep Agent (Abacus AI), and Block's AI Agent (the category leaders). Tools listed here emphasize **agentic capabilities** (multi-agent collaboration, real-time decision-making, risk-aware execution, backtesting, and adaptive strategies) rather than simple signals or charts.

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, local LLMs, multi-agent trading frameworks, quantitative finance, and full transparency/control over trading logic and data.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS Products

### Core Platforms (AI Financial Trading Agents)

- **[BlackRock Aladdin](https://www.blackrock.com/aladdin)**  
  Industry-leading investment management platform with advanced AI capabilities for portfolio construction, risk management, trading, and operations. Manages trillions in assets with AI-driven insights and execution support.

- **[Abacus AI Deep Agent](https://deepagent.abacus.ai/)**  
  Powerful general-purpose AI agent capable of complex financial tasks including equity research, market analysis, forecasting, and strategy development. Excels at multi-step reasoning for trading and investment workflows.

- **[TradingAgents](https://github.com/tauricresearch/tradingagents)** (with commercial extensions)  
  Multi-agent LLM framework simulating professional trading firms. Specialized agents for fundamental, sentiment, technical analysis, research, trading, and risk management.

### Advanced & Specialized Platforms

- **Block's AI Agent** (Block/Square financial tools with AI trading features)  
  AI-powered agents integrated into Block's ecosystem for payments, crypto, and trading-related decision support.

**Other notable mentions**: Various quant platforms with AI agents, robo-advisors with advanced automation, and enterprise solutions from traditional finance giants.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

### Dedicated Financial Trading Agent Projects

- **[TradingAgents](https://github.com/tauricresearch/tradingagents)**  
  Multi-agent LLM financial trading framework that simulates a professional trading firm. Features specialized agents (fundamental, sentiment, technical analysts, researchers, traders, risk managers) collaborating through debates and structured communication. Strong backtesting and performance results.

- **[FinRobot](https://github.com/ai4finance-foundation/FinRobot)**  
  Open-source AI agent platform for financial applications. Supports multiple specialized agents for equity research, algorithmic trading, risk assessment, and quantitative analysis using LLMs and reinforcement learning.

- **[AI-Trader](https://github.com/HKUDS/AI-Trader)**  
  Fully automated agent-native trading platform. Allows any AI agent to join, exchange ideas, and execute trades. Designed as a dedicated environment for AI trading agents.

- **[AgenticTrading](https://github.com/Open-Finance-Lab/AgenticTrading)**  
  Agentic trading framework bridging quantitative finance and cognitive AI. Enables reasoning, planning, and execution in trading pipelines with multi-agent orchestration.

- **[TwinMarket](https://github.com/TobyYang7/TwinMarket)**  
  Scalable behavioral and social simulation for financial markets using LLMs. Multi-agent framework focused on socio-economic modeling and market dynamics.

- **[PrimoAgent](https://github.com/ivebotunac/PrimoAgent)** (and similar multi-agent trading systems)  
  Multi-agent AI trading system with data collection, technical analysis, news intelligence, and portfolio management agents. Built with LangGraph for orchestration.

- **[LLM-TradeBot](https://github.com/EthanAlgoX/LLM-TradeBot)**  
  Multi-agent AI trading system using LLMs to optimize strategies and adapt to market conditions in real-time.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **FinRL** — Modular reinforcement learning library for automated trading and portfolio management.
- **OpenBB** — Open-source financial data platform with strong support for building AI trading agents and quantitative research.
- **FinGPT** — Open-source financial LLM foundation for building trading agents and sentiment analysis.
- General frameworks: LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen combined with trading libraries (ccxt, backtrader, vectorbt) for custom multi-agent trading systems.

**Frameworks for building custom agents**: Use LangGraph/CrewAI + FinRobot/TradingAgents templates + real-time data feeds (ccxt, Polygon, etc.) to create fully autonomous trading agents with risk management, backtesting, and paper/live trading modes.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Trading involves significant risk of loss. AI agents are tools for research and automation but **not substitutes** for professional financial advice, due diligence, or regulatory compliance. Always backtest thoroughly and use paper trading before live capital.
- Open-source projects may have varying levels of maintenance and should be audited before use with real money.
