### 🚀 What are AI Trading Agents?
**AI Trading Agents** are autonomous or semi-autonomous software systems that leverage Large Language Models (LLMs), Reinforcement Learning (RL), and Multi-Agent Orchestration to perform complex financial tasks. Unlike traditional "bots" that follow static rules, these agents possess **agentic capabilities**: they can reason, plan, collaborate, and adapt to shifting market regimes in real-time.

**Key Use Cases:**
*   **Autonomous Market Research:** Multi-step reasoning for technical and fundamental analysis.
*   **Agentic Sentiment Intelligence:** Real-time analysis of news, social media, and earnings calls.
*   **Risk-Aware Execution:** Adaptive order routing and real-time risk management.
*   **Portfolio Optimization:** Autonomous rebalancing and asset allocation using RL.

### 🎓 Getting Started with AI Agents in Trading
*Keywords: How to build AI trading bot, Agentic Finance tutorial, Python for AI Agents*

If you are new to the world of **Agentic Finance**, here is a recommended path to building your first autonomous agent:

1.  **Define your Workflow:** Identify if you need a single agent (e.g., for sentiment) or a **Multi-Agent System** (e.g., researcher + executioner).
2.  **Select a Foundation:** Start with a specialized model like **FinGPT** or a general-purpose LLM (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5).
3.  **Choose a Framework:** Use **LangGraph** for complex, stateful loops or **CrewAI** for task-oriented multi-agent collaboration.
4.  **Connect to Data:** Integrate with **ccxt** (crypto) or **OpenBB** (multi-asset) for real-time market data.
5.  **Implement Guardrails:** Ensure your agent has strict risk management logic and a "Human-in-the-Loop" approval step for live trades.

## ⚖️ AI Governance & Compliance
*Keywords: AI Governance in Finance, Model Explainability, Agent Audit Trails, EU AI Act Compliance*

As autonomous agents manage increasing amounts of capital, **governance** and **explainability** have become critical:
*   **Audit Trails:** Maintaining a deterministic log of an agent's reasoning process for regulatory review.
*   **Guardrails:** Real-time monitoring to prevent agents from exceeding risk parameters or violating compliance rules (e.g., wash trading).
*   **Explainable AI (XAI):** Tools to interpret *why* an agent made a specific decision, essential for institutional adoption and EU AI Act alignment.

## 🧠 Key Concepts in Agentic Finance

*   **Multi-Agent Systems (MAS):** Groups of specialized AI agents (e.g., a "Sentiment Agent" and a "Technical Agent") collaborating to improve decision accuracy.
*   **Agentic Workflows:** Moving from single-prompt interactions to iterative loops of planning, execution, and self-correction.
*   **Outcome-Driven Automation:** Assigning responsibility for high-level financial outcomes (e.g., "maintain delta-neutrality") rather than individual tasks.
*   **Agent Harnesses:** The underlying memory and state management layers that coordinate multi-agent fleets.
